# Manuscript to teaching slides

Establish the operation, audience/viewing conditions, editability, language arrangement,
content coverage and any fixed slide count. Infer what sources/conversation establish;
ask only about unresolved choices that materially change the work. Sermon and lecture are
content modes, not mandatory aesthetics.

Inspect actual sources: slide dimensions, native objects vs flattened images, fonts, style
provenance and known defects. Preserve DOCX emphasis and parenthetical terminology. Keep
meaningful later edits and speaker emphasis in an existing deck. `project.py init` retains
hashed copies and extracts segments; it does not decide source authority or prove coverage.
Choose authority or reconcile manuscript/deck differences in `project.json`. Retain earlier
source snapshots when revisions arrive.

Identify thesis, question, evidence, argument, transitions, application and conclusion.
Preserve important qualifications when condensing. Track substantive omissions, paraphrases
and additions. Set `content_policy`: full content on slides, summary with notes, or a user choice.
For each major argument, consider diagrams, imagery, paired arrangement, staged disclosure
or a sequence of pages. Record source IDs, page IDs, relationship, treatment and key rationale
in a short storyboard. A familiar layout is not evidence for a relationship.

Choose style from the user's reference, project preset or a suitable new direction. Maintain
coherent type, spacing and visual grammar within the project. Example dimensions/density are
not universal limits. Restructure or split before reducing readability. Set CJK/Latin fonts
explicitly and inspect exports. In the bundled 1920×1080 style, CSS px convert to 0.75 points
on a 20×11.25 inch PPT canvas; scale proportionally for other physical dimensions.

Use the host's presentation authoring/rendering workflow. Use native text, tables and diagrams
where editability is requested. Library previews are references, not an editable layout
implementation. The private source deck retains original objects and known defects. Copy
before reusing; do not overwrite the reference.

Review whole-deck argument/coverage, relationship, quote fidelity, bilingual pairing and rhythm.
Render all pages and inspect dense/changed pages at full size. Check line-start punctuation,
stranded title characters, closing punctuation and font substitution. Automated text bounds
are candidate findings to inspect, not a count of proven visual defects.

Record output/source hashes and review method. XML, renderer, native PowerPoint, room projection
and speaker feedback are distinct evidence. Fix genuine defects without repeatedly rewriting
effective content. Route meaning changes to the author. Import a finished teaching deck into
worship assembly with its own style preserved.
